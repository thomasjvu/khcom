# Third character — white/green hair, pistol

Unnamed original adult female character: long white hair with neon green
underside and tips, red turtleneck sweater, black leggings, black sneakers,
dark pistol. Created with the built-in image generation tool.

- `starter-sheet-v1.png`: 20 idle/walk poses, front/back right and left.
- `combat-sheet-v1.png`: 24 pistol attack poses, four directional views,
  plus six muzzle flash, shot streak and impact effect concepts.

Art drafts only. The combat generation retained a gray/gold background even
after a transparency edit request; background removal remains necessary.
Both sheets need native pixel cleanup, anchor normalization, indexed GBA
palette conversion and animation/OBJ metadata before ROM import.
Exact generation prompts are in `generation-prompts.txt`.
